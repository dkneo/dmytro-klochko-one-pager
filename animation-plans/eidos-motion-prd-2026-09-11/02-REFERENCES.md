# Reference board and what to borrow

These are principles and interaction references, not visual templates to copy.

## Primary references

| Reference | What is excellent | What Eidos borrows | What Eidos does not copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Apple Human Interface Guidelines: Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion) | Feedback follows gestures, stays brief, remains cancellable and avoids burdening frequent actions | Direct card manipulation, short response, no waiting for animation | Apple’s glass aesthetic |
| [2026 Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/) | The Interaction winner is praised for effortless controls; Delight winners reward memorable responses around simple actions | Perfect one-gesture comprehension before decorative personality | Award-page styling or platform chrome |
| [Moonlitt design interview](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=v1nphz91) | Complex visual information is simplified into quiet, precise interactions | Calm motion that serves rich imagery rather than competing with it | Lunar visual motifs |
| [CapWords design story](https://developer.apple.com/articles/capwords) | Waiting is integrated into the meaningful transformation from captured object to collected sticker | The artwork visibly becomes a filed specimen while it loads or saves | Stickers, confetti and childlike bounce |
| [Cosmos introduction](https://help.cosmos.so/en/articles/11846092-intro-to-cosmos) | A saved visual contributes to a person’s “visual DNA”; moodboards remain visually led | The real thumbnail moves into a real collection and color neighborhood | Generic masonry-board branding |
| [Cosmos discovery](https://help.cosmos.so/en/articles/11717949-discovery) | Similarity is legible through style, color, mood and composition | Use the artwork’s own color to tint feedback and its destination | Algorithmic similarity as an animation gimmick |
| [Rive state machines](https://rive.app/blog/how-state-machines-work-in-rive) | Motion is bound to actual states and can react immediately to live data | Named loading, dragging, threshold, committed and error states | Adding a runtime dependency merely to play linear clips |
| [Rive loading components](https://rive.app/blog/state-machines-make-iteration-a-breeze-for-designers-and-developers) | A loader can respond to real progress and finish cleanly when data arrives | The registration loader has start, waiting, progress/ready and finish states | Endless unrelated mascot loops |

## The quality distinction

Award-level motion is not “more animated.” It is more inevitable. The user should understand the result at full speed, feel that it came from their gesture, and never notice the system waiting for a prerecorded clip.
