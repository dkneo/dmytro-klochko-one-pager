# UI-05: session completion, no ceremony

Do not animate saved thumbnails into a mini moodboard. That repeats the old collage problem and delays the user's destination.

When the queue ends:

- replace the stage in 140ms;
- show `you kept {n} works` and a direct `see the moodboard` action;
- show one large kept work if available, not a grid;
- keep Faun as a small registered mark;
- navigate immediately when asked.

For zero keeps, show `nothing stayed this time · keep looking` with no animation.

Reduced motion uses the same resting state with no transition.
