# Handoff checklist

## Meaning

- [ ] A first-time viewer understands that Eidos is for collecting visual art.
- [ ] The active artwork is the largest and most colorful object.
- [ ] Faun reads as the stable product identity.
- [ ] No invented score, trait, title or personality diagnosis appears.

## Source integrity

- [ ] Exact Faun pixels and silhouette are preserved.
- [ ] Exact artwork pixels are preserved apart from one documented crop.
- [ ] Artist, title, year and source are live data.
- [ ] No generated website background is presented as user art.
- [ ] No duplicate or near-duplicate work is selected.

## Motion

- [ ] Keep, pass and undo complete within 220ms.
- [ ] Keyboard judgment never queues ornamental motion.
- [ ] Absolute favorite completes within 500ms and never blocks the next card.
- [ ] Loader is absent under 700ms, registers once and holds.
- [ ] Every transition can stop or retarget from its current state.
- [ ] Reduced motion preserves state and meaning.
- [ ] No video is required for UI feedback.

## Layout and performance

- [ ] Desktop is reviewed at 1366, 1440, 1920 and 2560px.
- [ ] Mobile is separately composed and reviewed at 390px.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow.
- [ ] Art remains identifiable at each viewport.
- [ ] First paint includes a complete poster, never a blank media box.
- [ ] Offscreen and hidden-tab animation is paused.
- [ ] No layout shift when art decodes.

## Cost

- [ ] No Fal call happened before storyboard approval.
- [ ] Any optional texture spend remains at or below $4.
- [ ] Every call, rejection and exact cost is logged.
