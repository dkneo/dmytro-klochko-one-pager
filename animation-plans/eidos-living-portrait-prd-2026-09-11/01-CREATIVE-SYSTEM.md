# Creative system: the registered self

## Product proposition

Eidos is a beautiful, endless moodboard of visual things a person genuinely loves. It does not infer a personality score. It lets the collection remain the evidence.

The signature image is a printing relationship:

- **the artwork is the color plate;**
- **Faun is the black key plate;**
- **the user supplies the pressure by choosing.**

The visual answer to “what does this product do?” is one work becoming visible through Faun, followed by Faun resolving sharply over it.

## Character roles

- **Faun notices.** He is the primary product mark and the fixed geometry of the living portrait.
- **Gryphon remembers.** It appears only for provenance, current location, map and archive actions.
- **Art is the subject.** It receives the greatest area, strongest color and longest hold.

## Composition rules

1. Show one active work at a time.
2. Give the work at least 58 percent of the hero visual field on desktop and 62 percent on mobile.
3. Keep the work identifiable. Avoid anonymous micro-crops.
4. Let Faun occupy 62 to 82 percent of the visual field height, depending on crop.
5. Use the large open pink circle as registration geometry, never as an enclosing logo badge.
6. Use one orange point as a rare registration accent.
7. Keep live text outside generated or raster motion.

The archive can be large. The hero must not visualize size by becoming denser. Time communicates growth: a new chosen work changes the plate while Faun remains.

## Palette

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Paper | `#f2e2c9` |
| Warm board | `#ead8bb` |
| Graphite | `#292723` |
| Raspberry | `#a8425d` |
| Baby pink | `#ff9bc0` |
| Orange point | `#ff5f24` |

Artwork color is not remapped to the brand palette. Pink is a printing plate and control state, not ambient decoration. Orange is under 1 percent of a frame. No teal or brand-blue creature.

## Motion grammar

### Routine interface response

- press: 80 to 100ms;
- keep or pass completion: 160 to 220ms;
- undo: 180ms or immediate for keyboard;
- no motion queues;
- every transition can retarget from its current state.

### Rare authored response

- absolute favorite: 410 to 500ms;
- first-load hero reveal: at most 1.2 seconds after the page is already usable;
- loader: hidden for the first 700ms, then one registration and a hold;
- no autonomous repeating hero loop.

### Physical behavior

- color plate may land 2 to 3px out of register;
- black key catches it with a decisive strong ease-out;
- paper and camera stay still;
- Faun never blinks, breathes, waves, morphs or floats;
- avoid bounce, elastic overshoot, orbit, parallax and particle reward.

## Tone

The system should feel like a contemporary print studio handling treasured works: tactile, exact and a little strange. It must not resemble a CMYK sample sheet, a luxury mythology campaign, a children’s collage, a generic culture magazine or mascot entertainment.
