# QC scorecard

Score each criterion from 0 to 5. Anything below 4 is rejected. Total must be at least 46 of 50 before review.

| Criterion | 0 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product clarity | Decorative mythology | Collecting visual art is understood in five seconds |
| Artwork respect | Tiny, anonymous or recolored | Large, identifiable, exact and credited |
| Character integrity | Redrawn or acting | Exact Faun geometry used as a printing plate |
| Visual hierarchy | Copy and decoration compete | Art first, Faun second, controls third |
| Motion coherence | Mixed effects and reward tropes | Contact, pressure, transfer, key, settle everywhere |
| Responsiveness | Motion queues or blocks input | Immediate, interruptible and retargetable |
| Loader honesty | Premature infinite mascot loop | Hidden under 700ms, finite registration, hold |
| Mobile composition | Desktop crop | Separate useful composition at 390px |
| Accessibility | Meaning depends on motion | Reduced motion and live status preserve meaning |
| Performance | Video-heavy, blank first frame | Complete poster, responsive sources, paused offscreen |

## Mandatory rejection regardless of score

- multiple small art fragments in the hero;
- thumbnail flight, orbit, halo, confetti or sparkle;
- blue dominant creature;
- generated text or redrawn artwork;
- routine feedback over 220ms;
- loader loop or flash on fast requests;
- anonymous crop;
- claim of completion without viewing the actual rendered surface.

## Review evidence

Deliver desktop and mobile recordings at normal speed, a 0.25x motion audit, a reduced-motion recording, source-lock difference images, viewport measurements, dropped-frame trace and any Fal spend log.
