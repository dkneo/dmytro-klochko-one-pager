# What changed from the failed pack

| Before | V3 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eight generated videos | No required generated video | UI must respond to real state and input |
| Many artwork fragments around Faun | One large exact work at a time | Art is the product, not mascot decoration |
| Three vague portrait states | One clear state relationship | The user can understand cause and effect |
| Mascot animation | Static source-locked character plates | Identity remains exact |
| Multiple effect grammars | Contact, pressure, transfer, key, settle | Every response feels related |
| Loader appears at 400ms and cycles | Nothing before 700ms, one registration, then hold | Fast navigation stays fast and waiting feels finite |
| Favorite thumbnail flies away | Art stays large; Faun receives a second pink pass | Rare delight without shrinking the chosen work |
| Session thumbnails gather into another collage | Literal count and direct moodboard action | Completion remains useful |
| Mobile inherits desktop | Separate 390px composition | Art and controls keep their intended scale |
| $20 generated-media cap | $0 required, $4 optional material cap | Spend only on a proven material gap |

The previous pack remains in `negative/` only as evidence of what must not ship.
