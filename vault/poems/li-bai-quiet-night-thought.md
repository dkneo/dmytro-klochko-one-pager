title: 静夜思

translator: "translated for this page, not a published version"
english: |-
  Before my bed, the bright moon's light —
  I take it for frost on the ground.
  I lift my head to look at the moon,
  lower it, and think of home.
note: |-
  Twenty characters. The first poem most children in China learn by heart, and still the one they can say at eighty.
---

床前明月光，
疑是地上霜。
举头望明月，
低头思故乡。
