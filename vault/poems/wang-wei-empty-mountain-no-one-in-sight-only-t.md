english: |-
  There seems to be no one on the empty mountain…
  And yet I think I hear a voice,
  Where sunlight, entering a grove,
  Shines back to me from the green moss.
note: |-
  Twenty characters that have been translated into English more than a hundred times, no two alike. Nobody agrees where the light is coming from.

空山不見人，但聞人語響。
返景入深林，復照青苔上。
