summary: |-
  Three tests before anything moves: what is it for, how often will someone see it, and is it fast. Something hit two hundred times a day should barely move at all, and keyboard-initiated actions should never animate, because the delay reads as the software being slow rather than the animation being pretty. Under 300ms or it is not responsive.
